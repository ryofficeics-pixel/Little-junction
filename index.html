Your much bigger railway.

The wooden layout has 7 times the original area. Explore forest bends, two river bridges, mountain grades and a real tunnel. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap ⌂ to reset.

Press Cab view or C to sit inside the Juniper Express. Drag to look around; ⌂ looks ahead. The camera follows curves and climbs with the locomotive. Press the same view button to return to the table.

The express accelerates gently, slows into curves and stops at the platform. Choose Bypass to skip the station. Route changes wait until the entire train is safely clear.

Your shunter loads at a siding, backs into the headshunt and goes out again. Choose 01, 02 or 03 to complete the next little job. New points apply when the train clears the junction.

Crossing gates and road traffic work automatically. Try night mode, sound the train horn, or click a tree for a tiny surprise.
