A whole world to ride.

This world has 50 times the area of the original Highlands map, with the same size trains. Explore forest serpentines, tundra horseshoes, frost-lake S-bends, farmland chicanes and the Frostfall tunnel. The Alpine Spiral climbs through a full turn; the central Skyweave figure-eight crosses above itself on supported viaducts. The winding circuit is about 2.4 times longer within the same map.

⇄ Train or T cycles through four mainline trains and the yard shunter. Choose a specific train from the selector or fleet list. Throttle, speed, horn and camera always belong to your selected train.

Cab view or C puts you inside the locomotive. Switch trains without leaving the cab. Drag to look around. ⌂ looks ahead. The shunter looks forward from its cab while reversing.

Map or M shows the entire landscape. Tap a train label to follow it. The small route map shows all five trains at all times.

Lane A / Lane B requests a crossover at the next available junction. Scenic requests lane B and the orchard branch at the Farmland junction. A request made after a route is locked waits for the following junction. Signals protect occupied sections and keep points locked until every wagon has passed.

Passenger trains stop at stations. Freight runs through. The independent yard shunter serves three sidings and reverses back before a new siding takes effect. Pause stops every train.

Each train has its own horn chord and rhythm. Press H or the horn button. Horns work independently of the ambience toggle; tunnel horns echo.
